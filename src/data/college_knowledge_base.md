## 2. Stories, Projects & Campus Experience 🌟
### Campus Life & Culture
Life at JNTU-GV CEV is vibrant and multi-faceted. Students engage in state-level technical symposiums, coding competitions, hackathons, annual sports meets, and cultural festivals. The campus hosts active student units for NSS (National Service Scheme), EDC (Entrepreneurship Development Cell), WEC (Women Empowerment Cell), and IEEE / CSI professional student chapters.

### Alumni Community & Networking
The official JNTU-GV Alumni Portal (https://alumni.jntugv.edu.in) links graduates and current students. It facilitates mentorship, career guidance, alumni networking, and alumni interaction events.

### Key Innovations & Student Projects
- **AI-Powered Precision Agriculture**: IoT sensor networks and drone imagery classification developed by CSE & ECE final year teams.
- **Solar Energy Storage Optimization**: Renewable microgrid research led by EEE department in collaboration with state power utilities.
- **Industrial Automation & Robotics**: CAD/CAM & Robotics prototypes designed by Mechanical Engineering students.
- **Novel Materials & Metallurgical Research**: High-entropy alloys and powder metallurgy projects published in international journals by Metallurgy scholars.

### Placement Stories & Statistics
- **Highest Package**: Up to 42 LPA.
- **Average Package**: 4.5 – 7 LPA.
- **Key Recruiters**: TCS Ninja/Digital, Wipro, Infosys, Accenture, Cognizant, L&T Technology Services, Hexaware, Tech Mahindra.
- **Training & Placement Cell (TPO)**: Organises intensive aptitude training, coding practice in Python/C++/Java, soft skills workshops, and mock interview rounds starting from 3rd year 1st semester.

## 4. Academic Regulations & Guidelines 📚
- **R20 Regulation**: Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), 160 Total B.Tech Credits, mandatory 10-week summer internships, Minor / Honors degree paths.
- **R23 Regulation**: Outcome-Based Education (OBE) following NEP 2020 guidelines, mandatory community service projects, skill courses.
- **R25 Regulation**: Next-gen curriculum integrating AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, and hands-on laboratory experiences across all streams.
